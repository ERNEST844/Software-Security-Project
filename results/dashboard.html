Held-out evaluation summary (2025 CVE cohort)

KEV is a sparse, uncertain exploitation signal; PoC existence is a denser but imperfect proxy. Recall in the top 5% is the share of future-positive CVEs found in the top five percent of the ranked list.

Interpretation and limitations
  • Exposure means metadata indicates an affected dependency version; it does not prove vulnerable code is reachable.
  • KEV listings and public PoC creation are proxies, not direct observations of all exploitation.
  • Priority tiers and actions come from a transparent policy, not an automated proof of required remediation.
  • Findings without a CVE may be ranked using severity-only evidence; the expanded record identifies the exploitation score source.
